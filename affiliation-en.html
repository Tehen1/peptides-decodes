How we make money — and what we refuse to sell.

Yes, we recommend products and some links are affiliate links. We own it plainly: when a peptide has real evidence and is legal over the counter, we tell you where to buy it (RC Peptides) and we earn a commission. When it doesn't, we tell you to avoid it — even when that would pay more. Here's the rule, no spin.

The rule: we only relay what we'd recommend without a commission.

An affiliate link means a purchase made through it may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you. It changes neither our analysis nor our verdicts. A product only appears as a link if it meets three cumulative conditions:

  • It has real human evidence (a cited RCT or meta-analysis).
  • It is legal to sell over the counter in the EU.
  • It is consistent with what our issues conclude.

If a product fails any of the three, it gets no link — whatever the commission on offer.

RC Peptides partnership — currently the only active affiliate partner

Our only active affiliate partnership is with RC Peptides. It covers 183 products from their catalogue, bounded by our editorial criteria. This section explains exactly what is covered, how tracking works, and what remains excluded.

What is included

  • Topical cosmetic peptides: Argirelin, Matrixyl, GHK-Cu.
  • Bioregulators / cosmetic or wellness supplements when supported by human data or tolerated OTC.
  • Compatible logistics accessories presented with honest lab context.

How tracking works

  • Clicks are tracked through a Cloudflare Pages Function.
  • Only anonymised data is stored: slug, newsletter ID, hashed IP, user agent, referrer.
  • No raw IP address is stored.
  • Data is logged in Supabase for analytics.
  • Each product link includes ?ref=PEPTIDESDECODED.

What is excluded

  • No unapproved injectable peptides are relayed through this partnership.
  • No product without human evidence is linked.
  • No claim is made beyond what the cited evidence supports.

Transparency: some links in our issues are affiliate links to RC Peptides. If you purchase through them, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. We only link products we would recommend regardless of commission.
