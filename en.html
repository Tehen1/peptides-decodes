Our editorial rule

"Written" is not "proven."

What this newsletter will never do

The web is saturated with peptides sold as miracles by influencers. We take the opposite path — and we own it:

  • No figure gets published without its primary source cited.
  • No figure ever borrowed from one study and pinned on another product: collagen data proves nothing about an injectable peptide.
  • An effect that's "significant in marketing" but weak in pooled analysis is labelled as such.
  • Never any promotion of unregulated injectable peptides (BPC-157, TB-500 & co. have no rigorous human safety data) — no "animal data" badge sneaks them in through the back door.
  • No medical advice. For any therapeutic molecule: medical consultation, full stop.
